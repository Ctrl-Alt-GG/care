## Before the Event

**Do I need to bring my own Ethernet cable?**
No. We provide a cable at every seat.

**Can I bring a laptop instead of a desktop?**
Yes. Bring a USB-C or USB-A to Ethernet adapter if your laptop has no built-in LAN port.

**What games will we play?**
Check Discord (`#announcements`) and the lobby portal for the current event's game list. Bring games you want to play in free time — you're not limited to official servers.

**Is there Wi-Fi?**
Yes, but it's for phones and casual browsing. Use the wired connection for gaming.

**Is there somewhere to sleep?**
Yes. There is a designated sleeping area. Bring a sleeping bag, camping mat, pillow, earplugs, and a sleep mask.

## At the Venue

**Where do I find my seat?**
Seating is first come, first served. Pick any open spot and set up. If you have accessibility needs, let staff know.

**Where are the toilets / showers?**
Ask at check-in or look at the venue map posted at the entrance.

**Can I bring food and drinks?**
Yes. Keep energy drinks and liquids away from keyboards. Clean up after yourself.

**Is there catering?**
We provide limited snacks and drinks (cold soft drinks, beer, sometimes coffee). For full meals, use Foodora or Wolt delivery — see [Food & Drink Guide](/wellness/food-and-drink/).

**Can I smoke / vape?**
Outside only. No vaping at your desk.

## Network

**Why is my internet slow?**
Pause Steam, Windows Update, OneDrive, and any other background downloads. See [Bandwidth Etiquette](/network/bandwidth-etiquette/).

**Why can't I see the game server?**
Check [I Can't See the Server](/network/cant-see-the-server/). Start with: cable plugged in, DHCP enabled, firewall allowing the game.

**Can I use a VPN?**
Turn your VPN off at the event. VPNs break LAN server discovery and slow down your connection. If you need a VPN for a specific reason, ask staff.

**Why is Wi-Fi not working for gaming?**
Wi-Fi is shared and unreliable for gaming. Use the wired cable at your seat. See [Wi-Fi vs LAN](/network/wifi-vs-lan/).

## Hardware

**My PC won't turn on — what do I do?**
Start with [PC Won't Start](/hardware/pc-wont-start/). Most issues are loose cables from transport.

**No sound from my headset — how do I fix it?**
See [Audio Setup & No Sound Fixes](/hardware/audio-setup/). The most common fix is setting the headset as the default output device.

**My PC is overheating — what should I do?**
See [Overheating: Symptoms and Quick Fixes](/hardware/overheating/). Give your rig breathing room and check temps with HWiNFO.

## Software & Games

**A game won't launch — help?**
Try [Game Launcher Fixes](/software/game-launcher-fixes/). If it's an anti-cheat error, see [Anti-Cheat Issues](/software/anti-cheat-issues/).

**I get "version mismatch" when trying to join a server.**
See [Version Mismatch](/software/version-mismatch/). Make sure your launcher is in Online Mode and your game is fully updated.

**How do I download games fast on-site?**
We have a local cache server — just start the download normally in Steam/Battle.net and it will automatically use the fast local copy. See [Where Downloads are Hosted](/software/local-downloads/).

## Tournaments

**How do I sign up for a tournament?**
Through the lobby portal: [lobby.ctrl-alt-gg.hu](https://lobby.ctrl-alt-gg.hu/). See [Tournaments & Team Sign-up](/logistics/tournaments/).

**My teammate disconnected mid-match — what happens?**
The match pauses. They can rejoin. The admin decides if they can't. See [What to Expect During a Tournament](/logistics/during-the-tournament/).

## Safety & Rules

**Someone is bothering me. What do I do?**
Come to the Organizer Desk or DM staff on Discord. You don't need proof — just tell us. See [Being a Decent Human](/wellness/being-a-decent-human/).

**There's a medical emergency. What do I do?**
For major emergencies, call **112** immediately and shout for staff. See [Emergency & Safety](/wellness/emergency-contacts/).

**I found something that isn't mine. What do I do?**
Bring it to the Organizer Desk (Lost & Found).
