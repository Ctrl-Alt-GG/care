## Find your match

1. Open [Bracket](https://bracket.ctrl-alt-gg.hu) and find the tournament. When logged out, the home page lists open tournaments.
2. Click **Schedule** on the tournament card. Look for your team, opponent, time, and **court** assignment. A court represents a playing area or station in the tournament; confirm its physical location with the organizers.
3. The tournament's **Overview** page shows current and upcoming matches under **Playing now** and **Coming up next**.
4. If your match has no time or court yet, ask the organizers. An empty schedule does not necessarily mean your entry is missing: the draw or scheduling may not be ready yet.

Check the time again before you play, as delays may change it. Bracket displays the schedule; it does not automatically connect you to a game server.

## Results and progression

- **Results:** recorded match scores. If your match has finished but no result appears, the organizer may not have saved it yet.
- **Public dashboard > Bracket:** the bracket, or group matches and tables. Use this to follow progression through an elimination stage.
- **Public dashboard > Standings:** overall team wins, draws, and losses. Confirm detailed qualification rules and tiebreakers with the organizers.
- **Public dashboard > Courts:** matches arranged by court.

**Public dashboard** is only available when sharing is configured and enabled. If it is missing or the link fails, ask the organizers for the correct address. You can still check the separate **Schedule** and **Results** links.

## Put it on a shared display

Open an enabled **Public dashboard**, then select **Big screen**. For court assignments, use **Courts**. On a shared display, use the public view rather than a signed-in organizer's management workspace.

## A result is wrong

Note the tournament name, both teams, the round, and the correct score, then tell an organizer. As a player or spectator, do not create a new tournament to correct it. The organizer workflow is covered in the [score-entry guide]({{< relref "record-tournament-results.md" >}}).
