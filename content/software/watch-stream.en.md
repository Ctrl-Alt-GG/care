## Open a live channel

1. Connect to the event network, or use the network access provided by the organizers. Streams is intended for a private network, not as a public video-sharing site.
2. Open the Streams address provided by the organizers. Browsing and playback do not require an account.
3. Choose a live channel from **All channels**, or open the featured stream with **Watch now**.
4. For video, unmute the player: playback starts muted. For radio, click **Listen live**.

Use headphones so you do not disturb nearby players. The video player's controls also let you switch to full screen.

## The stream will not play

- **The site itself is unreachable:** check that you are on the correct network. The private service may not be reachable over mobile data.
- **The channel is offline:** the broadcaster is not currently sending a stream. Wait for them to go live, then reload the page.
- **The status cannot be confirmed, or a warning says the data is stale:** the catalog has not received fresh data from the streaming server. This alone does not prove the broadcaster stopped. Try again later and contact the organizers if it persists.
- **There is video but no sound:** unmute the player, then check the browser tab's audio and your system's output device.
- **The browser cannot play the stream:** try an up-to-date browser. If another live channel works, tell the organizers which stream is affected.

When reporting a problem, include the channel page's address, the time, and the visible error message. Do not send passwords or publishing credentials.
