## Enter and confirm your team

[Bracket](https://bracket.ctrl-alt-gg.hu) manages tournament rosters, matches, and results. **Creating an account is not tournament registration:** a tournament manager adds players and teams; there is no self-service join button.

1. Tell the organizers which tournament you want to enter and the name you will use.
2. For a team game, provide the team name and each player's in-game name. For an individual tournament, ask how you will appear in the draw.
3. Ask for confirmation that you have been added to the correct team. Your Bracket name should be easy to match to your in-game name.
4. Once matches have been created, check your team's name, opponent, and time using the [tournament viewing guide]({{< relref "follow-tournament.md" >}}).

If you are missing from the draw or assigned to the wrong team, tell an organizer. Creating another user account will not fix it.

## When do I need a Bracket account?

You do not need to log in to view publicly available schedules and results. You need an account if you are helping organize a tournament or the organizers specifically ask you to create one.

1. Open **Log in** in Bracket.
2. Choose **Create account** if it is available.
3. Fill in **Display name**, **Email**, and **Password**. Use at least 12 characters for the password, and do not reuse a password from another service.
4. Click **Create account**. Successful registration signs you in and returns you to the home page.

Having an account does not automatically grant management access to other people's tournaments. If **Players**, **Teams**, or **Stages** is missing, or you see a read-only access message, contact the tournament organizer.

## I cannot log in

- If **Create account** is missing or you see **Registration unavailable**, registration is disabled on the server. Ask the organizers for help.
- Log in with your email address, not your display name.
- The **Password reset** page currently does not offer self-service password recovery. Contact the organizers about a forgotten password rather than waiting for an automatic email.
- When using a shared computer, log out when finished and do not save your password in the browser.
