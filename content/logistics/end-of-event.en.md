The last hour is just as important as the first. A rushed pack-out causes forgotten gear, broken hardware, and a messy venue.

## Before You Start Packing

- **Finish your current game.** Don't abandon a live match on your team.
- **Say your goodbyes.** Exchange Discord tags and socials with people you'd like to stay in touch with.
- **Take a photo of your setup** before you start unplugging — it helps if you can't remember which cable goes where.

## Step-by-Step Pack-Out

### 1. Clear Your Desk
- Throw away all your trash (food wrappers, cans, cups).
- Collect your personal items: phone, wallet, keys, charging cables.

### 2. Unplug in Reverse Order
1. Unplug peripherals from your PC (mouse, keyboard, headset).
2. Unplug the monitor cable from the GPU.
3. Unplug power cables (PC first, then monitor).
4. Unplug the Ethernet cable. **Leave it coiled neatly at your seat** — it belongs to the venue.

### 3. Return Borrowed Items
- Did you borrow a cable, mouse, or keyboard from someone?
- **Return it now.** Don't pack it by accident.
- If you borrowed something from the organizer, bring it to the Organizer Desk.

### 4. Pack Your Hardware Safely
- **If you brought your PC in a carry bag or case, use it.** Transport is when most hardware gets damaged.
- Wrap delicate items (GPU, monitors) in bubble wrap or clothing if you didn't bring proper packaging.
- Coil your cables loosely — tight bends damage cables over time.

### 5. Check the Lost & Found
- Look under your desk and chair — small items fall and hide.
- Check the Organizer Desk for anything you left behind earlier in the event.
- If you find something that isn't yours, hand it to the organizer.

### 6. Leave Your Area Clean
- Push in your chair.
- Make sure there are no cables left on the floor (trip hazard for the next person packing).
- If you used a sleeping area, fold up your sleeping bag and remove all your belongings.

## Departure Logistics

- **Parking:** Move your car only once you are fully packed and ready to go. Don't block the entrance while making multiple trips.
- **Carpooling:** If you're waiting for a carpool, move your gear to the side so others can pack out freely.
- **Public transport:** Check live schedules before heading out — last buses/trains may be earlier than expected.

## After You Get Home

- Post your highlights, photos, and moments in `#highlights` on Discord.
- If you had a great time (or a problem), let us know in `#general`. Feedback helps us improve the next event.

See you at the next one.
